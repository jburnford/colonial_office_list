## PATTERN ANALYSIS

**Root causes of over-extraction:**

1. **Page running headers** - Parser treats "COLONY_NAME." on continuation pages as new colony starts
2. **Section headers** - Administrative sections (EXECUTIVE COUNCIL, THE PARLIAMENT) treated as colonies
3. **Trade tables** - EXPORTS sections appearing 5 times treated as separate entries
4. **Person names in lists** - LOUIS BOTHA in treaty signatories mistaken for colony
5. **Geographic subdivisions** - Cities (DURBAN, ADELAIDE) and districts treated as colonies

**Pattern:** Parser relies only on "COLONY_NAME." format without checking for standard colony sections (Situation/Area, History, Constitution).
