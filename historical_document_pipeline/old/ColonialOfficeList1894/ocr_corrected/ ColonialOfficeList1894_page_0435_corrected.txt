RULES AND REGULATIONS.

NOTICE OF NEW LIGHT OR ALTERATION OF LIGHT.

Name of Light.
Place.
Latitude.
Longitude.
Number of Lights and Relative Position.
Colour of Light.
Fixed, or Flashing; Interval, and Period of Revolution in each.
Time when each Light is shown.
Colour or Peculiarity of Harbour Light, if any.
Height of Light above High Water.
Height of Lantern above the level of the Sea at High Water.
Character and Order of Optical Apparatus.
Description of Fog-signal, if any.

Signature and Position of Informant.

N.B.—In addition to the above form, information respecting the intention (or the danger) it is intended to avoid; the position of the lighthouse in respect to some natural mark; a small plan of the subject coast; and the number of the chart; the position, name, and description of the keeper’s dwelling, and any information that Address, “Secretary of the Marine Department, Board of Trade,” Whitehall, London.
