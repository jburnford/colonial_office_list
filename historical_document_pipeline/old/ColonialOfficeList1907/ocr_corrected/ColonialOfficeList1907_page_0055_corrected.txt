EXCHEQUER AND AUDIT DEPARTMENT.

COLONIAL AUDIT BRANCH.

The accounts of certain Crown Colonies and Protectorates are audited by the Comptroller and Auditor-General, J. A. Kempe, Esq., C.B., acting as Honorary Auditor on behalf of the Secretary of State for the Colonies.

The accounts of the British Central Africa, the East Africa (including the Uganda Railway), the Uganda and the Somaliland Protectorates, are also examined in the Colonial Audit Branch by direction of the Lords Commissioners of the Treasury.

The examination of these accounts is conducted in accordance with the Colonial Audit Regulations (Revised April, 1903), as approved and confirmed by the Secretary of State for the Colonies.
