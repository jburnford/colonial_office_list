By Census of 1891, 278,328.
Estimate on 31st December, 1905, 303,390.

The population of the colony was made up in 1891 of—Aborigines, 7,463; East Indians, 105,463; Chinese, 3,714; Portuguese, 12,166; Africans, 3,433; Europeans other than Portuguese, 4,558; Blacks, &c., 141,531. Of the total 170,106 were natives of the colony; males were 151,759; females, 126,569, and 10,000 Aborigines estimated.

Immigrant Population on Estates, 31st March, 1905.

Under Indenture. Not under Indenture. Children.
East Indians 11,385 39,995 19,263.

The approximate number of East Indian immigrants not residing on estates is 62,457. Total immigrant population, 132,990.

The aboriginal Indians actually within the settled districts of the Colony at the period of taking the Census of 1881 were found to number 7,708, but the numbers of the tribes within the British territories vary, and are at all times very uncertain.
