In 1905 there were 1,761 schools in operation, employing 2,272 teachers, of whom 597 were men, and 1,675 women.

Normal schools are provided for the training of teachers.

There is one University, styled “The University of Manitoba,” with examining and degree-conferring powers only. The affiliated colleges are St. John’s (Episcopal), St. Boniface (Roman Catholic), Manitoba (Presbyterian), Wesley (Wesleyan), the Manitoba Medical, and Brandon (Baptist) College.
