STRAITS SETTLEMENTS (FEDERATED MALAY STATES).

Kubu, on the F.M.S. Railway, to Kuala Lipis. There is a daily motor car service on this road. A branch road has also been made with Government assistance from Tras to Bentong, a distance of some 20 miles, for the purpose of opening up the mines in that district, and trunk roads from Bentong to Kuala Pilah in the Negri Sembilan, and from Bentong to Kuala Lumpur by the Sempak Pass, are under construction.

In the Kuantan district a cart road is being made from the Blat River to the Lepar watershed to open up the tin fields of that part of the State.
