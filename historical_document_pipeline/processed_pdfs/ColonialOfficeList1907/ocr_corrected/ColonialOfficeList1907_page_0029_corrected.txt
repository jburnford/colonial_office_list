The Corporation buy and receive for collection Bills of Exchange; grant Drafts payable at the above Agencies and Branches; and transact general banking business connected with the East.

Deposits of Money are received for fixed periods at rates which may be ascertained on application, and on Current Accounts Interest is allowed at 2 per cent. per annum on the Minimum Monthly Balances, provided they do not fall below £200.
