AUSTRALIA—VICTORIA.

Legislative Assembly.

Parliament was elected on 1st June, 1904.

First Session opened June 29th, 1904.

Sixty-five Members representing 65 Electorates, and 2 Members representing the Railways and 1 the Public Service. Number of Electors, 243,702.

Speaker, Frank Madden.

Chairman of Committees, A. W. Craven.
