There is weekly mail communication with Great Britain, vidâ Quebec in summer, and Halifax in winter, fortnightly with the West Indies and Demerara, and four a week vidâ New York. There is a through daily service by the Canadian Pacific Railway to the Pacific coast, a monthly direct steam service with Australia (with the exception of Western Australia), vidâ Vancouver, and once every three weeks vidâ San Francisco, and a tri-weekly steam service with Japan and China, viâ Vancouver, from April to September inclusive, and once every four weeks during the rest of the year.

There is a parcel post to Newfoundland, West Indies, Australia, United Kingdom, Continental Europe, Hong Kong, and Japan.

Defence.

There is an Imperial naval station at Halifax, N.S., which is strongly fortified, and garrisoned by a Canadian force. The defence of the Dominion rests with the local forces. The Canadian Militia since confederation has, in accordance with the Canadian Militia Act, been under the immediate command of a Major-General of the English Army. The Dominion is divided, for military purposes, into twelve districts, with a District Officer Commanding, and a Brigade Staff in charge of each. The active Militia numbers about 55,765 men. There are two royal schools of artillery (one field, one garrison); two schools of cavalry, and one of mounted rifles, and five of infantry, and the Dominion Government in 1876 opened a school of scientific military instruction at Kingston, known as “The Royal Military College,” with a staff of 12 professors.
