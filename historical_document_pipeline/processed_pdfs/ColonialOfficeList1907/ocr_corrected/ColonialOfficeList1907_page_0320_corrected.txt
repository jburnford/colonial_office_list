Civil Establishment.

Note.—All salaries over 100l. a year are by Laws No. 11 of 1875 and No. 32 of 1903 subject to a deduction of 4 per cent. payable to a widows’ and orphans’ fund, and officers must contribute 2 per cent. of their salaries to entitle them to a pension on their retirement.
