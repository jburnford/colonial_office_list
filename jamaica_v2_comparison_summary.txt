CRITICAL BUGS FOUND
-------------------

🚨 BUG #1: Pattern5 Role Inheritance (CRITICAL)
   Severity: CRITICAL
   Frequency: 28% of all records (7 out of 25)
   Impact: Causes role accuracy to be 44% instead of 72%

   Problem: When extracting names from lists, Pattern5 inherits the role
            from a PREVIOUS line instead of using the role from the CURRENT line.

   Example:
   Line 357: Cashier, T. B. Hendricks, £200.
   Line 358: Clerks, 1st Class, C. W. Chapman, and E. W. Astwood, £200.
             ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
   Expected: E. W. Astwood → "Clerks, 1st Class"
   Actual:   E. W. Astwood → "Cashier" ❌ (from line 357!)

   Fix: Extract role from SAME line as names
   Impact if fixed: +11.2 points → 84.4/100 ✅ MEETS TARGET!
