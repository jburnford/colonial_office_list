## Colony Information

**Location**: South American mainland, north-eastern coast
**Capital**: Georgetown (formerly Stabroek)
**Area**: 76,000-83,000 square miles (increased after 1938 boundary survey)
**Status**: Crown Colony → approached independence as Guyana (May 26, 1966)

### Geographic Divisions
- **Demerara** (county) - includes Georgetown
- **Essequibo** (county) - largest county by area
- **Berbice** (county) - includes New Amsterdam

### Key Features
- Only British colony on South American mainland
- Three counties formed from Dutch settlements
- United in 1831 from three separate provinces
- Rich natural resources: sugar, gold, bauxite, forests
- Notable landmark: Kaieteur Falls (741 feet, 5x higher than Niagara)

## Constitutional Evolution Tracked

### Phase 1: Dutch/Early British (1814-1891)
- Court of Policy (5 official + 5 elected)
- Combined Court (Court + 6 Financial Representatives)
- College of Electors (abolished 1891)
- Limited franchise based on property

### Phase 2: Reformed System (1891-1928)
- Executive Council created
- Direct voting introduced
- Court of Policy becomes legislative body

### Phase 3: Legislative Council (1928-1961)
- Court of Policy & Combined Court abolished
- Legislative Council: 10 official + 19 unofficial members
- Extended franchise (1944)

### Phase 4: Self-Government (1961-1966)
- Bicameral legislature (1961-1964)
- Universal adult suffrage
- Council of Ministers
- Unicameral House of Assembly (1964)
- Independence May 26, 1966
