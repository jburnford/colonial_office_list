## Key Findings Across All Years

### 1. CONSTITUTIONAL EVOLUTION

#### Phase 1: Traditional Crown Colony (1867-1909)
- **Executive Council:** 5 members (Governor, Colonial Secretary, Queen's/King's Advocate, Treasurer, Auditor-General, Commanding Officer)
- **Legislative Council:** 15 members (all appointed, 6 unofficial members)
- **Government:** Entirely Crown-appointed, no elected representation
- **Notable Event:** 1833 constitutional reforms established dual council system (mentioned in historical sections)

#### Phase 2: Limited Representation (1910-1920)
- **Constitutional Change (1910-1911):** Introduction of elected members
  - Legislative Council expanded to 21 members
  - First elected unofficial members (4 elected, 6 nominated unofficial)
  - Communal representation: 2 Europeans, 1 educated Ceylonese, 1 Burgher
- **Significance:** First step toward representative government

#### Phase 3: Expanded Self-Government (1920-1930)
- **Manning Reforms (1920-1924):** Major constitutional advancement
  - Legislative Council: 49 members total
    - 34 elected unofficial members (territorial + communal constituencies)
    - 5 ex-officio, 7 nominated official, 3 nominated unofficial
  - Executive Council: 9 members (4 ex-officio, 5 others including 4 unofficial)
  - Special electorates: European (urban/rural), Commercial, Burgher, Indian, Muslim, Ceylon Tamil
- **Significance:** Substantial Ceylonese participation in governance

#### Phase 4: Donoughmore Constitution (1931-1946)
- **State Council System (1931):** Revolutionary change
  - 50 elected members (territorial constituencies)
  - 3 Officers of State (Chief Secretary, Attorney General, Treasurer) 
  - 8 Governor-nominated members
  - **Universal Suffrage:** All citizens (male and female) aged 21+ could vote
  - **Ministers:** 7 elected chairmen of executive committees styled "Ministers"
  - **Board of Ministers:** Replaced Executive Council (Ministers + Officers of State)
- **Significance:** Ceylon became first British colony with universal adult suffrage

#### Phase 5: Move to Independence (1946)
- **Soulbury Constitution (1946):** Path to self-government
  - Parliament with Senate and House of Representatives
  - Cabinet system under Prime Minister
  - Three offices of state abolished
  - "Virtually fully self-governing in internal affairs"
- **Independence:** Ceylon gained full independence February 4, 1948

## Data Quality Issues Identified

### OCR Challenges
1. **Name Variations:** Same person sometimes appears with different initials (e.g., "W. C. Gibson" vs "W. G. Gibson")
   - **Solution:** Documented as name variants with notes
   
2. **Rupee Values:** Some large numbers have unclear comma placement
   - **Example:** "5,022,179" vs "50,22,179" (Indian numbering system)
   - **Solution:** Normalized to Western format, noted in provenance
   
3. **Diacritical Marks:** Tamil/Sinhalese names sometimes inconsistently rendered
   - **Example:** "Coomaraswamy" vs "Kumaraswami"
   - **Solution:** Preserved source spelling, variants documented

### Data Gaps
1. **1907-1908, 1912-1914, 1926, 1935, 1938-1945:** No CEYLON sections in these years' Colonial Office Lists
