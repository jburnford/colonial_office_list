## Key Historical Periods

### Period 1: British Protectorate (1878-1914)
- **Years Available**: 1883, 1886, 1888, 1894, 1896-1900, 1905-1911
- **Status**: British occupation under 1878 Constantinople Convention
- **Government**: High Commissioner with Executive and Legislative Councils
- **Key Features**: Annual tribute to Turkey (£92,800), locust control campaigns, development of infrastructure

### Period 2: British Crown Territory (1914-1923)
- **Years Available**: 1915, 1917-1923
- **Status**: Annexed to British Crown (November 5, 1914)
- **Key Event**: WWI service, annexation formalized by Treaty of Lausanne (July 24, 1923)

### Period 3: Crown Colony (1923-1931)
- **Years Available**: 1924-1925, 1928-1930
- **Status**: Formal Crown Colony (from May 1, 1923)
- **Government**: Governor (title changed from High Commissioner), Legislative Council with elected members
- **Development**: Railway expansion, harbor improvements, agricultural development

### Period 4: Direct Rule (1931-1946)
- **Years Available**: 1932-1934, 1936-1937, 1946
- **Status**: Legislative Council suspended after 1931 riots
- **Government**: Governor with Executive Council only
- **Key Event**: WWII service (1939-1945)

### Period 5: Post-War Period (1946-1960)
- **Years Available**: 1946, 1950-1960
- **Status**: Crown Colony with growing independence movement
- **Key Events**: 1950s EOKA campaign, 1959 Zurich-London Agreements, Independence August 16, 1960
- **Development**: Significant population growth, modernization
