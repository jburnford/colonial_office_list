## Key Historical Milestones

### 1643 - Discovery
- Tasman discovers Fiji Islands

### 1874 - Cession to Britain
- October 10, 1874: Deed of Cession signed
- Chief Thakombau (also spelled Cakobau) cedes sovereignty to Britain
- Charter issued establishing Fiji as Crown Colony

### 1875 - Measles Epidemic
- Devastating epidemic kills ~40,000 inhabitants
- Population reduced by over 25%

### 1880 - Rotuma Annexation
- Rotuma annexed to Fiji by Letters Patent (December 17, 1880)
- Implemented May 13, 1881

### 1882 - Capital Transfer
- Seat of government moved from Levuka to Suva
- Suva chosen for superior harbor facilities

## Economic Development

### Key Industries:
1. **Sugar** (dominant from 1880s onward)
   - 1887: 12,831 tons exported from 12,250 acres
   - Multiple sugar mills established
   - Primary driver of Indian immigration

2. **Copra** (coconut product)
   - Consistent export throughout period
   - 1887: 1,957 tons from 19,939 acres
   - Rotuma specialty crop

3. **Gold Mining** (20th century)

## Administrative Structure

### Governors (Key Appointments):

2. 1875-1879: Sir Arthur Hamilton Gordon, GCMG (first substantive governor)
3. 1880-1885: Sir George William Des Voeux, KCMG
4. 1888-1897: Sir John Bates Thurston, KCMG (previously Colonial Secretary)
5. Multiple governors through to 1966

### Executive Council:
- Initially: Governor + 3-4 official members
- Later expanded: Governor + official members + unofficial members
- By 1950s: Mixed European, Fijian, Indian representation

### Legislative Council:
- Initially: Governor + 2+ nominated members
- Evolved to: Governor + 6 official + 6 unofficial members
- By 1920: Added elected members
- By 1963: 19 official + 18 unofficial members (6 European, 6 Fijian, 6 Indian)

## Extraction Methodology

### LLM-Based Extraction (NO PYTHON):
1. **Read source markdown files** using Read tool
2. **Analyze content** using Claude Sonnet 4.5 context-awareness
3. **Extract entities** following schema v2.0 specifications
4. **Apply controlled vocabularies** from master_vocabulary_filtered.json
5. **Generate provenance** with source files, line numbers, original text
6. **Write JSON outputs** using Write tool
7. **Validate** against schema requirements

### Entity Extraction Process:
- **Places**: Identify colonies, islands, cities, geographic features with coordinates when available
- **People**: Extract names, separate titles/honors, link to positions with salaries
- **Institutions**: Government bodies, councils, courts, departments
- **Economic Data**: Revenue, expenditure, trade, production figures with validation
- **Events**: Historical events with precise dating
- **Relationships**: GOVERNS, LOCATED_IN, PART_OF, HOLDS_POSITION, REPORTS_TO, etc.
