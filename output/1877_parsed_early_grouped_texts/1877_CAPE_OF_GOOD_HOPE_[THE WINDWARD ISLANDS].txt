CAPE OF GOOD HOPE.

Free passages by mail steamer are granted by the Colonial Government to railway labourers, artizans, &c., selected by the Agent in England for the service of the Government on railways and public works. Under the assisted Emigration, employers of labour can import hands on payment of 7L towards their passage, and the agent selects suitable persons and contracts with them, on behalf of the employers if desired.

The same privilege is given to artizans and others who have promises of employment in the colony.

Applications respecting passages should be addressed to W. Burnet, Esq., 15, Coleman Street, London, E.C.
