NATAL.

Free passages from Europe to Natal are granted by the Natal government to domestic servants, farm labourers, engineers, engine-drivers, carpenters, bricklayers, stonemasons, blacksmiths, wheelwrights, and shipbuilders, provided (1) that the immigrant is nominated by some person residing in the colony; (2) that the application is approved by the colonial government; and (3) that the Immigration Agent for Natal in London is satisfied that the immigrant is a person of good character and of proficiency in his professed trade or calling.

The nominor in the colony is required to make arrangements for the immediate reception in the colony of the immigrant introduced on his behalf, and to guarantee the immigrant employment on his arrival, at a stated rate of wages for at least one year, but not more than three years.

Further particulars can be obtained on application to the Emigration Agent for Natal, No. 79, Coleman Street, London, E.C.
