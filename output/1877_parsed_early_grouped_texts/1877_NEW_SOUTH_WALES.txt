NEW SOUTH WALES.

The selection of emigrants for passages to this Colony is limited to persons who can pay one-third of the cost, and are of sound health and good character. They must be either married couples not exceeding 40 years of age, or single men and unmarried women not exceeding 35 years of age. The classes eligible are mechanics, farmers, miners, vine-dressers, labourers, and domestic servants, and a few small working capitalists in any branch of colonial industry. A proportion of the immigrants, not exceeding 10 per cent. of the whole, may be German vine-dressers.

The following payments are required to be made by persons selected for passages to this Colony, viz., for bedding and mess utensils:

- 1 and under 12 years of age: 10s. each.
- 12 and not exceeding 40 years of age: 1l. each.

As contributions towards passage—

- 1 and under 12 years of age: 2l. 5s. each.
- 12 and not exceeding 40 years of age: 4l. 10s. each.

Applications for passages should be addressed to the Agent-General for New South Wales, 3, Westminster Chambers, Victoria Street, Westminster, S.W., from whom forms of application and all other information may be obtained.
