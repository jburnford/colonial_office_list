QUEENSLAND.

Emigration to Queensland is regulated by the "Immigration Act of 1872."

Assisted passages are granted under the following scale of payments to farmers, vine dressers, mechanics, and their families, and general labourers:

| Ages | Males | Females |
|------|-------|--------|
|      | Deposit for passage | Amount payable 12 months after arrival | Deposit for passage | Amount payable 12 months after arrival |
| Between 1 and 12 | £2 | £6 | £1 | £7 |
| Between 12 and 40 | £4 | £12 | £2 | £14 |
| Above 40 | £6 | £10 | £6 | £10 |

Colonial nominees are provided with passages upon payments ranging from £1l. to 4l. made to the Immigration Agent in Brisbane, or to Sub-Agents throughout the Colony by resident colonists.

The following is the scale of payments to be made in the Colony for nominated passages:—

Free passages are granted to female domestic servants and such persons of the farming class as may from time to time be specially required in the Colony. No payments, with the exception of a very trifling amount for ship's kit, are required from this class.

Applications for free and assisted passages are to be addressed to the Agent General for Queensland, A. M. McAlister, Esq., C.M.G., 92, Charing Cross, London, S.W.
