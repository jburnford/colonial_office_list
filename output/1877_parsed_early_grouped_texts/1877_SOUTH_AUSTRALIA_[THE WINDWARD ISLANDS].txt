SOUTH AUSTRALIA.

Free passages are at present granted to persons deemed by the Emigration Agent for the Colony to be eligible, as follows:

To single female domestic servants, artizans, agricultural, railway, and other labourers, and gardeners, copper, tin, and lead miners, not exceeding 40 years of age, single, or married with not more than three children.

Persons of the above callings and ages, paying their own full passages, receive a Land Order Warrant of the value of 20l. for every adult above 12, and 10l. for children between 1 and 12 years of age.

Further information respecting the issue of Land Orders and the grant of passages may be obtained on application to F. S. Dutton, Esq., C.M.G., Agent-General for the Colony, No. 8, Victoria Chambers, Victoria Street, S.W.
