WESTERN AUSTRALIA.

Free passages are granted to the nominees of residents in the colony, and to others of the following classes:

- Agricultural labourers, female servants, mechanics, miners and shepherds, of ages ranging from 18 to 40, able-bodied and healthy.

The emigrants pay 15s. each for bedding and mess-kit.

After two years' residence in the colony each adult emigrant is entitled to select, from any unimproved rural crown lands open to selection, 50 acres of land, and each emigrant between the ages of 16 and 21, 25 acres of land, not to exceed 150 acres in all for any one family. After three years' occupation, the land becomes the property of the selector, provided certain improvements have been made on it.

The emigrants are not required to enter into any agreement for repayment or otherwise. Passages are granted to families which do not comprise more than three children under 12 years old.

For further particulars application may be made to Messrs. Felgate and Co., 12, Clement's Lane, London, E.C.
