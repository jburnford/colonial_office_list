The Corporation buy and receive for collection Bills of Exchange; grant Drafts payable at the above Agencies and Branches; and transact general banking business connected with the East.



Deposits of Money are received for twelve months fixed at 4 per cent. per annum, for six or nine months 3½ per cent. per annum, and for three months 3 per cent. per annum, and on Current Accounts Interest is allowed at 2 per cent. per annum on the Minimum Monthly Balances, provided they do not fall below £200.
