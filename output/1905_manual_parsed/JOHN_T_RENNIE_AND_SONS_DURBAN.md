Comfort in Riding.



Messrs Thomas & Sons wish to draw the attention of Sportsmen, and especially those who are constantly in the saddle, to the many advantages of their



LACED KNEED RIDING BREECHES.



Buttons constantly get broken and are driven into the shin, and, in cases where the breeches are too tight or too loose, they admit of no adjustment. The use of laces obviously does away with these irritating accidents, and, in addition, gives an even elastic pressure unobtainable in any other way.



None but those who have been in the saddle for many hours at a stretch can conceive the relief this simple expedient alone affords. But, if any further proof were necessary, the fact that this method of fastening at the knees has proved of inestimable value in South Africa during the late war, and has been universally copied by other tailors, should be sufficient. It should be borne in mind, however, that the majority of these imitations are unsatisfactory, and are calculated to prejudice the merits of the properly made garment, which can only be obtained of us.



Ordering:—We shall be pleased to send patterns and instructions for self-measurement, free of charge by post anywhere.



Colonial Customers, and gentlemen residing out of town, need not hesitate to leave to us the selection of the cloth. Our experience teaches us that, as a rule, it is better to do so as far as possible, merely stating the requirements and whether the breeches are for incessant hard wear or only for occasional wear. To new customers we shall be glad to submit samples of cloth and prices with particulars of the measurements we require, which can be easily taken by any one.
