A WEEKLY JOURNAL FOR ALL INTERESTED IN SOUTH AFRICAN AFFAIRS.



SOUTH AFRICA, which was established twenty years ago, is published every Saturday, price 6d. It may be obtained at all Bookstalls and of all Newsagents, or direct from the Offices. The terms of Subscription are 29s. inland, and 39s. abroad per annum.



SOUTH AFRICA is conducted by EDWARD P. MATHERS, the well-known South African Journalist, who, in the early Eighties, persistently foretold the coming greatness of the South African Goldfields.



SOUTH AFRICA, on account of its Special South African Mining News and reliable advice regarding Shares, is eagerly read by all British and Continental Investors. It regularly publishes analytical articles dealing with South African Companies. They supply useful and unique statistical aids to Investors.



SOUTH AFRICA is the most profitable medium in which to advertise the Prospectuses of all new Companies. Tariff from £15 15s. The Reports and Accounts of all the leading South African Gold, Diamond, and other Companies are regularly advertised in SOUTH AFRICA. By adopting its pages for this purpose Directors ensure the greatest publicity possible for all information concerning their Companies.



The Head Offices of SOUTH AFRICA are at Winchester House, London, E.C.
