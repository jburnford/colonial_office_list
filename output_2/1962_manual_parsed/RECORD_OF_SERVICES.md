RECORD OF SERVICES

Public Services of Overseas Territories (including some members of Her Majesty's Overseas Civil Service, and other officers, serving outside the dependent territories who remain eligible for transfer), Colonial Office and Crown Agents for Oversea Governments and Administrations

The Governor or Governor-General is styled "His Excellency" while actually administering a Government, and an officer administering a Government in the absence of the Governor or Governor-General is similarly styled. All Judges are addressed out of Court as "The Honourable".

In order to save space the above titles are omitted in these notes and it is possible to include, owing to considerations of space, only those officers who are permanent holders of administrative, judicial, professional and technical appointments and who have had ten years' service, except in a few cases of holders of important posts. CORBIN, M. H. A.—b. 1916; ed. Queen's Royal Coll., Trin.; barrister-at-law; mag., Trin., 1945; registr. and marshal, 1954; puisne judge, 1957.

CORDERY, R. D.—b. 1923; ed. Christ's Hosp. and St. John's Coll., Oxford; mil. serv., 1942-45; lieut.; D.O., Basuto., 1947; admin. offr., Uga., 1958; senr. D.O., 1961.

COREE, J. P.—b. 1921; ed. St. Michael's Coll., Limerick, Nat. Univ. of Ireland, and Purdue Univ., U.S.A.; exec. engr., S. Leone, 1947; senr., 1954; asst. D.P.W., Aden, 1958.
