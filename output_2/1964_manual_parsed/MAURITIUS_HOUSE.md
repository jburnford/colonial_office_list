MAURITIUS HOUSE
16 Upper Montagu Street, W.1.
Telephone: AMBassador 2481.
Acting Secretary—J. M. Drummond.
Mauritius House was founded during the second world war by Viscountess Kempsley for the benefit of Mauritians serving in the Forces. It now carries out welfare work, particularly among students, and acts as an information centre for the Colony.
Mauritius House is financed by the Government of Mauritius but is controlled by a private company, which until January, 1951 operated under the War Charities Act and is now incorporated under the Companies Act, 1948.

OFFICE OF THE COMMISSIONER FOR NORTHERN RHODESIA

Estate House, Haymarket, S.W.1.
Telephone: WHItchall 5858.
Cables: Norhodcom London.
Commissioner—J. P. Murray, C.M.G.

The functions of the Office are to carry out the instructions of the Northern Rhodesia Government and to co-operate with the Colonial Office and the Crown Agents for Oversea Governments and Administrations as occasion demands; to provide general information and publicity and to further the interests of Northern Rhodesia and its people.

The Office assists in the recruitment of staff for the Government and local authorities.
