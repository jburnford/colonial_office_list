**THE EAST CARIBBEAN CURRENCY AUTHORITY**

P.O. Box 620C Roebuck Street,
Bridgetown, Barbados.

The East Caribbean Currency Agreement between the Governments of Antigua, Barbados, Dominica, Montserrat, St. Kitts-Nevis, St. Lucia and St. Vincent signed in January, 1965 provides for the establishment of a common currency and an authority to issue and manage that currency in the territories of the participating Governments. The Authority also acts as agent of the participating Governments for implementing their obligations under the British Caribbean Currency Board Agreement, 1964. The new Authority began to issue new currency in October, 1965.
