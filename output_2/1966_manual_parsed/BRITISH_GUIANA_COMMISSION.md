A British Guiana Government Office was established in 1962, its main function being the reception and welfare of migrants from British Guiana and the general welfare of Guianese students in the United Kingdom.
In April 1965, the Office was designated the British Guiana Commission, and the first Commissioner for British Guiana in the United Kingdom, Sir Lionel Luckhoo, was appointed.
The scope of the Office has been extended to all fields as the Official Representative of the British Guiana Government.
