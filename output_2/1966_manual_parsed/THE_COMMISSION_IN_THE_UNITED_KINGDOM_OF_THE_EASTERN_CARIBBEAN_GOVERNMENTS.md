With the dissolution of the Federation of The West Indies and the closure of the Office of the Commissioner for The West Indies, British Guiana and British Honduras, the eight Governments of Antigua, Barbados, Dominica, Grenada, Montserrat, St. Kitts, St. Lucia and St. Vincent established their own office in London. MAURITIUS COMMISSION
153/156 Grand Buildings, Trafalgar Square, W.C.2.
Telephone: WHItehall 2895/6.
Cables: Maurcom, London, W.C.2.
Commissioner—Dr. L. Teelock.
Secretary—R. Chasle.
The Mauritius Commission was established on the 12th May 1964. The main functions of the Commission are the promotion of trade, industry and tourism; the dissemination of general information on Mauritius; the exploration of the U.K. employment market possibilities with a view to assisting Mauritians to emigrate; and the welfare generally of Mauritians visiting or residing in the U.K. The Commission has, with effect from the 1st July 1965, assumed administrative control of the office for Students which is now known as its Students' Division and exercises an oversight on behalf of the Mauritius Ministry of Education on matters affecting students. The Commission also works in close collaboration with the Colonial Office, the Ministry of Overseas Development and the Crown Agents for Oversea Governments and Administrations, and keeps in touch with other institutions and organisations, the role of which is to promote the development of the Commonwealth and the welfare of its peoples.
