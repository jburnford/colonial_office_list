MAPS

The Directorate of Overseas Surveys, part of the Ministry of Overseas Development, undertakes topographical mapping of both dependent and independent Commonwealth countries overseas. It has published a large number of medium-scale contoured maps which in addition to giving details of town locations, roads, and physical features, show the location of cultivated areas together with details of natural vegetation.

These topographical maps at 1:50,000, or larger scales have been published for most of the dependent territories including the West Indian islands, Mauritius, Seychelles, Basutoland, Falkland Islands and Fiji; in addition specialist maps illustrating land use, geology, or soils have been published of certain areas.

Most of the overseas territories have also been mapped on one sheet at a considerably larger scale than can be found in most atlases.

Full details of the Directorate's publications are given in their catalogue. The agents for the sale of the Directorate's maps and catalogue are: Edward Stanford Ltd., 12-14, Long Acre, London, W.C.2.
