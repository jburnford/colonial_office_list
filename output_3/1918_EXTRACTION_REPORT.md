## World War I Context & Post-War Mandates

### Captured German Colonies (Referenced but NOT yet mandates)

This 1918 list captures a critical transitional moment. Several former German colonies had been captured during WWI but were not yet formally established as League of Nations mandates (which would occur in 1919 following the Treaty of Versailles):

#### 1. **Togoland** (Former German Togo)
- **Status in 1918:** Captured German colony
- **Administration:** Referenced in Gold Coast and Nigeria sections
- **Future:** Would become British and French mandates in 1919

#### 2. **Cameroons (Kamerun)**
- **Status in 1918:** Captured and under joint administration
- **Document Quote:** "The German Colony of the Cameroons, or Kamerun, has been captured and is now being administered by the French and British Governments. The British sphere is being administered by the Government of Nigeria."
- **Future:** Would become British and French mandates in 1919

#### 3. **German East Africa**
- **Status in 1918:** Military operations ongoing/territory captured
- **Administration:** Multiple references to British military administration
- **Future:** Would become British mandate of Tanganyika (1919); small portions to Belgium (Ruanda-Urundi)

#### 4. **German South-West Africa**
- **Status in 1918:** Captured by South African forces
- **Administration:** Under South African military control
- **Future:** Would become South African mandate in 1919

### Important Notes on Mandates

- **No formal League of Nations mandates exist yet** - WWI ended November 11, 1918, but the Treaty of Versailles wouldn't be signed until June 28, 1919
- **Pre-mandate period** - Captured territories were under military or provisional civil administration
- **1919 Colonial Office Lists** would be the first to formally list mandate territories
- The **League of Nations mandate system** was established by Article 22 of the Treaty of Versailles
