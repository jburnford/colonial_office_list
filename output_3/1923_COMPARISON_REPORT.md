## Key Changes from 1922 to 1923

### 1. Dominions Excluded
The four self-governing Dominions (Australia, Canada, New Zealand, South Africa) are no longer included in the Colonial Office List, reflecting their increasing autonomy.

### 2. Mandate Territories Added
League of Nations mandate territories are now formally included (Palestine, Tanganyika).

### 3. Administrative Consolidation
- Northern and Southern Rhodesia combined under single "RHODESIA" entry
- Some smaller territories may have been consolidated or moved to appendices

### 4. Missing Territories Require Investigation
- Straits Settlements and Federated Malay States not found in main sections
- Bechuanaland not found as separate section (despite being a High Commission Territory)
- Seychelles only appears in bibliography section
- Western Pacific High Commission not found as main section

## Conclusion

The 1923 Colonial Office List shows significant organizational changes from 1922, primarily reflecting:
- **Exclusion of self-governing Dominions** (Australia, Canada, New Zealand, South Africa)
- **Inclusion of League of Nations mandate territories** (Palestine, Tanganyika, Togoland)
- **Administrative consolidation** (Rhodesia)
- **Possible reorganization** of some Asian territories (Straits Settlements, Federated Malay States)

The actual number of territories under British colonial administration may not have decreased as dramatically as the colony count suggests, as many territories were reorganized or reclassified rather than lost.
