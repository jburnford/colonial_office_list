## Major Changes from 1923

### 1. Rhodesia Split (1923 → 1924)
- **1923:** RHODESIA (combined Northern and Southern)
- **1924:** NORTHERN RHODESIA only
- **Reason:** Southern Rhodesia became self-governing in October 1923 and was no longer under Colonial Office administration

### 2. New Separate Entries in 1924
The following territories appeared as separate entries in 1924 (some may have been included in other sections in 1923):

- **BECHUANALAND PROTECTORATE** - Now listed separately (High Commission Territory)
- **GILBERT AND ELLICE ISLANDS COLONY** - Separated listing
- **NEW ZEALAND DEPENDENCIES** - Separated from New Zealand administration
- **NEWFOUNDLAND** - Added (had responsible government but financial difficulties led to return to Colonial Office administration)
- **SEYCHELLES** - Separated listing (previously may have been grouped differently)
- **STRAITS SETTLEMENTS** - Listed (Singapore, Penang, Malacca, Dindings, Cocos Islands, Christmas Island)
- **UNFEDERATED MALAY STATES** - Listed (Johore, Kedah, Perlis, Kelantan, Trengganu)

### 3. Federated Malay States
- **1923:** Listed as separate entry
- **1924:** Integrated into STRAITS SETTLEMENTS section
