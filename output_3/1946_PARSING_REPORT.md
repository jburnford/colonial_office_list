## DOCUMENT STRUCTURE NOTES

### Part II Organization (Line 2623 - 15609)

The 1946 list has a different structure than pre-war editions:

1. **Part II A**: Introduction and general colonial information
2. **Part II B**: Historical and Statistical Account of Colonies (starts line 2667)
   - Individual colony sections with:
     - Situation and Area
     - Climate
     - General Description
     - History
     - Constitution
     - Population
     - Administration
     - Religion
     - Currency and Banking
     - Communications
     - Social Services
     - Public Revenue and Expenditure
     - Governors
     - Councils

3. **Part III**: Staff Lists (starts line 15610)
   - ALL colonies have staff lists
   - 8 territories ONLY have staff lists

### Preface Notes

From the 1946 Preface:
> "With the conclusion of hostilities consideration was given to the question of the revival of the work of reference, which was issued up to 1940 under the title of 'The Dominions Office and Colonial Office List.'"

> "The paper restrictions and the need for early publication, arising from the fact that no List has been issued since 1940, have made it necessary to omit from the present edition the biographical notes and certain other sections..."

> "The next edition, in a more comprehensive form, including biographical notes, will appear in 1948, after which the List will be issued annually."

**IMPORTANT:** This is explicitly stated as the FIRST EDITION of a new series, published in a hurry post-war.

### Global Situation

1. **WWII ended:** May 8, 1945 (Europe), September 2, 1945 (Japan)
2. **British Empire status:**
   - Many territories occupied by Japan 1941-1945
   - Major reconstruction needed

   - Fall of Singapore (Feb 1942)
   - Japanese occupation of Hong Kong, Malaya, Singapore, North Borneo, Sarawak

1. **Military to Civil Administration:**
   - Hong Kong: Civil admin restored May 1, 1946

   - Singapore: BMA continued
   - Sarawak: Transferred to Crown Colony status (1946)
   - North Borneo: Crown Colony status (1946)

2. **Mandates and Protectorates:**
   - Trans-Jordan: Independence 1946
   - Palestine: Mandate continuing (but tensions high)
   - Tanganyika: UN Trusteeship replacing League of Nations mandate
